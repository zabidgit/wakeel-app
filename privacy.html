Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 26, 2026

Wakeel is built on a simple principle: your data belongs to you. Unlike other AI assistants, your conversations and personal information never leave your private server. We can't read your messages because we don't have access to them.

How Wakeel Works

Wakeel is a personal AI assistant that runs on your own private server. The Wakeel iOS app connects directly to your server — we are not an intermediary. This means:

  • Your conversations are processed on your server, not ours
  • Your personal data (calendar, health, reminders) stays on your device and your server
  • We have no ability to read, access, or analyze your conversations
  • There is no central database of user conversations

What We Collect

We collect the minimum required to operate the app:

  • Expo Push Token: A device identifier used solely to deliver push notifications. This token is stored on your private server, not on our infrastructure.
  • Crash Reports: Anonymous, aggregated crash data through Expo's standard error reporting to help us fix bugs. No personal data is included.
  • App Store Analytics: Standard Apple App Store analytics (downloads, sessions) that Apple provides to all developers. We have no control over this.

That's it. We do not collect your name, email, conversations, location, health data, contacts, or any other personal information.

What We Don't Do

  • We never train AI models on your data
  • We never sell, share, or monetize your personal information
  • We never access your conversations or files
  • We never track your location or browsing activity
  • We never show advertisements
  • We have no analytics SDK that profiles your behavior

AI Processing

When you send a message to your Wakeel, it is processed by a large language model (such as Claude by Anthropic) via your server's API connection. This means:

  • Your messages are sent from your server to the AI provider — not from us
  • The AI provider's data handling is governed by their API terms (e.g., Anthropic's API does not use customer data for training)
  • We are not a party to this exchange and have no visibility into it

Push Notifications

To deliver notifications when the app is closed, your device's Expo Push Token is registered with a push service running on your private server. Notifications are routed through Apple's Push Notification service (APNs) and Expo's push relay. The content of push notifications is generated by your Wakeel on your server.

Data Storage

  • On your device: Chat history, pairing credentials (encrypted via iOS Keychain), and app preferences are stored locally on your iPhone.
  • On your server: Conversation history, memory files, and agent configuration are stored on your private server infrastructure.
  • On our infrastructure: Nothing. We do not operate servers that store your data.

Your Rights

Because your data lives on your device and your server, you have full control at all times:

  • Delete messages: Use the "Clear Messages" option in Settings to delete all local chat history
  • Disconnect: Disconnect from your server at any time from Settings
  • Delete everything: Uninstall the app to remove all local data. Delete your server to remove all server-side data.
  • No account required: Wakeel does not require an email, phone number, or any personal information to use

Children's Privacy

Wakeel is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Security

Communication between the Wakeel app and your server uses encrypted WebSocket connections (WSS/TLS). Pairing credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain, Apple's most secure on-device storage. Your server is protected by your own security configuration (firewall, SSH keys, authentication tokens).

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the revised version here with an updated date. Material changes will be communicated through the app or our website.

Contact
